THE IMPORTANT DETAILS

Privacy policy.

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Ayesha Web Studio (“we”, “our” or “the studio”) respects your privacy. This page explains what happens when you browse this website or contact us through its available links and forms.

1. Information you choose to provide

If you complete the project enquiry form, the details you enter are used to prepare a message and open WhatsApp. The website does not submit the form to a studio-owned database or store your enquiry on a website server. You decide whether to send the message in WhatsApp.

If you contact us through a social platform, email or another third-party service, that service may process your information under its own privacy policy.

2. How information is used

Information you voluntarily send may be used to respond to your questions, understand project requirements, communicate about a potential project and provide requested services.

3. Cookies, analytics and third-party services

This website does not intentionally set its own analytics or advertising cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and links to third-party platforms such as WhatsApp and social networks. Those providers may collect technical information according to their own policies. Their services and policies may change independently of this website.

4. Data sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal information. We do not receive or retain information through a server-side contact form on this static website. Messages you send through third-party platforms remain subject to those platforms’ systems and retention practices.

5. Security

Reasonable care is taken in how this website is built, but no internet transmission or third-party service can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Children

This website is intended for general business enquiries and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website or its practices change. The updated date at the top of this page will indicate the latest revision.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact Ayesha Web Studio via WhatsApp or use the contact section.

Note: This is a starter privacy policy for a simple static portfolio website, not legal advice. Update it if you add analytics, advertising pixels, payment processing, newsletter tools or server-side forms.
